Tom’s Google Drive

A private, owner-operated connection that lets authorized agents on an Emergent VPS work with the owner’s Google Drive files, Google Docs, Gmail, and Google Calendar.

The connection supports finding and reading files, creating and updating files, reading and editing document tabs, managing authorized email tasks, and working with calendar events. Agents act only on tasks authorized by the account owner.

This is a personal-use application, not a public service. Signing in does not make account data public.
