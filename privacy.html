Privacy policy

Tom’s Google Drive is a personal-use connection operated by the Google account owner. Updated September 20, 2026.

Data accessed and purpose

The application uses Google account identity information to select and verify the connected account. It uses Google Drive and Docs access to list, read, create, edit, and organize files and document tabs for tasks authorized by the owner. Gmail access supports reading and managing messages, labels and drafts, and sending messages only when the owner requests it. Calendar access supports reading the owner’s calendar list and reading, creating, updating and deleting events only for authorized tasks. The Google Drive permission technically permits deletion and access across the connected account; agents must follow the owner’s task permissions.

Storage and processing

OAuth credentials are stored in a protected location on the owner’s VPS. Authorized VPS execution identities share that connection. Authorized agents may process selected account content through the agent and model services selected by the owner to perform the requested task. This application does not publish Drive, email or calendar contents through these informational pages.

Credentials are retained while the connection is active. Task outputs and operational records are retained under the owner’s instructions. The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and contact

The owner can revoke access in Google Account → Third-party connections and request removal of locally retained credentials and task data. Revoking access does not itself remove existing task outputs or backups. Contact the account owner through the support address shown on the Google consent screen.
